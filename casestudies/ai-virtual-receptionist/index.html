AI VIRTUAL RECEPTIONIST

Video, Voice & Chat Reception Automation

I developed an AI Virtual Receptionist, an intelligent automation solution built with TrueGen AI and n8n for hospitals, clinics, educational institutions, salons, corporate offices, and service centers. The platform handles inbound video calls, voice calls, and chats, enabling appointment booking, availability checks, and information requests through natural conversations.

With Google Calendar integration, the system provides real-time scheduling, appointment confirmations, and seamless interaction management. This solution reduced manual receptionist workload, improved response times and customer experience, and provided a scalable virtual reception system that can be easily adapted across multiple industries.

TOOLS USED

TrueGen AI n8n Slack Google Sheets Webhooks

DISTINCTIVE FEATURES

Natural Conversational Interaction

Engages users through human-like voice, video, and chat conversations, understanding requests naturally while delivering accurate and contextual responses.

FAQ & Interruption Handling

Answers frequently asked questions instantly and intelligently handles interruptions, allowing users to switch topics without disrupting the conversation flow.

Appointment Automation

Automates appointment booking, availability checks, rescheduling, and confirmations through seamless Google Calendar integration with real-time updates.
